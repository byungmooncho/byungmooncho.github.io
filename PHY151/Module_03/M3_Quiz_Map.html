How to keep this organized

One ledger per module

PHY151_M3_Item_Ledger.json in Question_Pools is the single list. It holds every claim and every item, wherever the item came from. This page, the coverage matrix and the D2L package are all built from it. Nothing is tracked anywhere else.

Every item points at one claim

An item is filed under its claim ID (N8.5), never under a textbook section. A claim with no items is a hole. A claim with eight or more items is over-served. Neither needs a judgment call to spot.

Every item has one status

draft → scored → variants → live for new work. keep / repair / retire for inherited items. You review one note at a time: click its row, score its drafts, and the status moves.

Sources: M3 claim-level coverage audit and matrix (25 Sept 2026), item→claim map, and the Tier-1 drafts file. Counts treat duplicates as coverage, as the audit did. Earlier N5 and N9 rung-2 drafts are not yet fully mapped to claims; only N9 items 2-11 and 2-11b (for N9.5) show on the map so far.
