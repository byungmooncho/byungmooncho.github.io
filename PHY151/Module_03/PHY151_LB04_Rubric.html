Lab 4 Rubric: Newton's Second Law

Out of 100 points. This is the rubric on the LB4-M3 assignment in Brightspace. Lab 4 page · Word copy · PDF copy

Use this rubric as your writing guide: the criteria follow the lab report template top to bottom. Full-credit descriptors say exactly what a complete answer contains, so read the Complete column before you start writing, not after. Note 6 is where the physics for the Introduction and the Experimental section comes from — bringing it into the report is expected, and is marked below.

Grammar, spelling, and formatting problems are assessed 1 point per occurrence. Deadline: a report submitted by the deadline may be revised and resubmitted up to two more times. A report that arrives after the deadline but before grades are posted is graded as on time but cannot be revised; one that arrives after grades are posted is late, may take a penalty, and gets no detailed feedback.

The phrase “human error” earns no credit anywhere in the report. A percent error under 10% may be attributed to ordinary experimental uncertainty, provided you say so explicitly and support it with the uncertainty on your fitted slope. Above 10%, named and quantified error sources are required. An optional bonus problem on the handout is worth up to 3 points; it is added to the grade directly and is not a row on this rubric, so skipping it costs nothing.
