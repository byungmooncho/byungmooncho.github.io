Lab 0 Rubric: Graphing Activity (Hand Plot vs. Excel Plot)

CriterionPtsExcellentGoodDevelopingMissing / Inadequate
Hand Plot33 Axes scaled to fill most of the grid page; both labeled with units, Time (s) and Position (m), with evenly spaced ticks; descriptive title; all ten points plotted as a scatter plot, not connected.2 Plot complete, but one item slips: a missing unit or title, a cramped scale, or a point misplotted.1 Two or more items missing, or the points connected dot-to-dot, or the scale squeezed into a corner.0 No hand plot, or the photo is too blurry or dark to read.
Best-Fit Line and Slope22 A single straight best-fit line drawn with a ruler through the scatter; two well-separated points on the line circled and labeled; rise-and-run triangle drawn; slope = rise/run worked on the plot with the unit m/s.1.5 Line and slope present, but the two points are close together or are data points, or the unit is missing.1 Line drawn freehand or forced through particular points, or the slope given with no work on the plot.0 No best-fit line or no slope.
Excel Table and Graph22 Data table and scatter chart pasted as pictures, side by side and in line with text; chart has a title and axis titles with units; markers only; linear trendline showing its equation and R².1.5 Table and chart present; one element missing, such as R², an axis unit, or the chart title.1 Default Excel formatting, points joined by lines, no trendline equation, or the table or the chart missing.0 No Excel work.
Observe and Comment22 In sentences: states the Excel best-fit equation; reports the Excel slope and the hand-plot slope, both in m/s, as the speed; compares them (for example as a percent difference) and accounts for any gap.1.5 Both slopes reported with units, but the comparison is thin or the equation is missing.1 Only one slope reported, units missing, or no comparison.0 No comment section.
Format and Submission11 On the Word template, two pages or fewer, italic instructions deleted, name and date in the heading, submitted as one PDF named "Graphing Activity - Your Name.pdf".0.75 One slip: leftover instructions, a third page, or the file name.0.5 Several slips, or the template not used.0 Not submitted as a PDF, or unreadable.
