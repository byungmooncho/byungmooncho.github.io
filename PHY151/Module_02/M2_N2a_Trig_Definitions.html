Trig Definitions: From Drawings to Calculations

Synopsis In Note 2 we resolved vectors by drawing and counting grid squares. Trigonometry lets us skip the grid: in a right triangle with angle θ, sin θ = opp/hyp, cos θ = adj/hyp, and tan θ = opp/adj — so the opposite side is (hyp)(sin θ) and the adjacent side is (hyp)(cos θ). A vector and its two components form exactly this triangle: the vector is the hypotenuse, the x-component is the adjacent side, and the y-component is the opposite side. That gives the two most-used equations of this module: Fx = F cos θ and Fy = F sin θ. One warning before you compute anything: put your calculator in degree mode.
Performance Expectations By the end of this note, you will be able to…
  • PE1Name the sides of a right triangle relative to θ — hypotenuse, opposite, adjacent — and recognize that "opposite" and "adjacent" swap if you pick the other angle.
  • PE2State the trig definitions — sin θ = opp/hyp, cos θ = adj/hyp, tan θ = opp/adj (SOH-CAH-TOA).
  • PE3Compute the sides from the hypotenuse and angle — opp = (hyp)(sin θ) and adj = (hyp)(cos θ), with the calculator in degree mode.
  • PE4Resolve a vector with trig — Fx = F cos θ and Fy = F sin θ for a vector of magnitude F at angle θ above the +x axis.
  • PE5Recombine components into a vector — magnitude |F| = √(Fx² + Fy²) and direction θ = tan⁻¹(Fy/Fx).
  • PE6Check answers with friendly triangles — recognize the 3-4-5 and 6-8-10 patterns and the sanity checks at 0°, 45°, and 90°.

💡You already know this

Right triangles with a known angle are hiding all around you:

🪜 Ladder against a wall Same ladder, steeper angle → it reaches higher up (more "opposite") but its foot sits closer in (less "adjacent").
🌤️ Shadows Your height, the shadow's length, and the sun's angle form a right triangle — that's tan θ in the wild.
🛣️ Road grade signs "6% grade" is rise over run — the tangent of the road's angle, posted on a highway sign.

Three equations to remember

↔️ Fx = F cos θ The horizontal component is the side adjacent to θ — CAH.
↕️ Fy = F sin θ The vertical component is the side opposite θ — SOH.
🧭 |F| = √(Fx² + Fy²) Going backwards: Pythagoras for the magnitude, θ = tan⁻¹(Fy/Fx) for the direction — TOA.
