Lab 2 Rubric: Free Fall from a Ticker Tape

Out of 100 points. This is the rubric on the LB2-M1 assignment in Brightspace. Lab 2 page · Word copy · PDF copy

Use this rubric as your writing guide: the criteria follow the lab report template top to bottom. Full-credit descriptions tell you what a finished section contains.

Grammar, spelling, and formatting problems are assessed 1 point per occurrence. Deadline: a report submitted by the deadline may be revised and resubmitted up to two more times. A report that arrives after the deadline but before grades are posted is graded as on time but cannot be revised; one that arrives after grades are posted is late, may take a penalty, and gets no detailed feedback.

The lab is scored out of 100 on the order-2 fit alone. The optional velocity method in the last section of the handout is worth up to 3 bonus points on top of that; skipping it costs you nothing.
