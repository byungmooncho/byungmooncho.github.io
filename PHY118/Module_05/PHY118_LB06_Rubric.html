Lab 6 Rubric: Centripetal Force

Out of 100 points. This is the rubric on the LB6-M5 assignment in Brightspace. Lab 6 page · Word copy · PDF copy

Use this rubric as your writing guide: the criteria follow the lab report template top to bottom. Full-credit descriptors say exactly what a complete answer contains, so read them before you write rather than after.

Grammar, spelling, and formatting problems are assessed 1 point per occurrence, capped at 3 points for the report. A fault already scored under a criterion is not deducted again as a formatting occurrence. Deadline: a report submitted by the deadline may be revised and resubmitted up to two more times. A report that arrives after the deadline but before grades are posted is graded as on time but cannot be revised; one that arrives after grades are posted is late, may take a penalty, and gets no detailed feedback.

The phrase “human error” earns no credit anywhere in the report. A percent difference under 10% between the mass from your slope and the mass from the balance may be attributed to ordinary experimental uncertainty, provided you say so explicitly and name a mechanism. The intercept is not noise — it is evidence, and a report that ignores it loses marks in Discussion.
