Lab 3 Rubric: Projectile Motion

Out of 100 points. This is the rubric on the LB3-M2 assignment in Brightspace. Lab 3 page · Word copy · PDF copy

Use this rubric as your writing guide: the criteria follow the lab report template top to bottom. Full-credit descriptors say exactly what a complete answer contains.

Grammar, spelling, and formatting problems are assessed 1 point per occurrence. Deadline: a report submitted by the deadline may be revised and resubmitted up to two more times. A report that arrives after the deadline but before grades are posted is graded as on time but cannot be revised; one that arrives after grades are posted is late, may take a penalty, and gets no detailed feedback.

The phrase “human error” earns no credit anywhere in the report. A percent difference under 10% may be attributed to ordinary experimental uncertainty, provided you say so explicitly. Over 10%, you must name specific error sources and quantify them.
