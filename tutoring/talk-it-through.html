Talk It Through

A 10-minute conversation about Newton's three laws. You explain your thinking; it asks questions.

Talk It Through is an AI conversation partner I built for my college physics classes. It gives you three everyday situations, one for each law. For each one you make a prediction, explain why in your own words, and then it changes one thing about the situation and asks again.

It won't teach you, give hints, or tell you whether you're right. That's on purpose: the point is to find out what you really think, not to get the right answer out of it. We'll go over your conversation together in our next session.

At the end, the app tells you to upload your file to Brightspace. That's for my college students. You can skip that step and send the file to me instead.

?Good to know

  • A follow-up question doesn't mean you were wrong. Everyone gets follow-ups, whatever they answered.
  • Use your own everyday words, not textbook phrases or formulas.
  • If you still believe your answer, stick with it. Explaining why is the whole point.
  • Type stop any time to end the conversation. That's completely fine, and it still counts.
  • If it suggests going back over the "Module 3" notes, those are the Forces & Newton's laws pages on the Interactives page.
  • If you turn on Read Aloud, it reads out a technical line at the start. Just ignore it.
  • I can read your conversation, and Playlab stores it. Don't type your full name or other personal details.
