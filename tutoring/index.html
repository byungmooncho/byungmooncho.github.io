Byungmoon Cho

Physics tutor · Assistant Professor of Physics, Finger Lakes Community College

I teach physics at Finger Lakes Community College in Canandaigua, NY, and I tutor high-school and college students in physics.

Before FLCC, I taught Regents Physics at Canandaigua Academy (2022–2024) and chemistry at Monroe Community College and Pima Community College. I also spent several years in research, with postdoctoral work in ultrafast laser spectroscopy at CU Boulder / JILA, the University of Arizona, and IBS / Korea University.
